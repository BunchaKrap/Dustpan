Dustpan — icon directions
One base geometry, four treatments: a low wide scoop in the bottom half of the grid, front lip flaring out to meet the floor, a back wall for depth, and the handle rising from the back. Big Sur rounded-square, 1024 master. Each card repeats the same paths at 96 / 64 / 32 / 16 px to check Finder and menu-bar legibility.
1a
Sweep
warm gradient · sparkles
The house direction — warmest, most "utility app". Sparkles thin out below 64 px and the back wall drops at 16 so the scoop stays crisp.
1b
Paper
light ground · pan + brush
Quietest of the four and the most native next to Finder. The brush sits clear of the pan on the left and drops entirely at 32 px and below.
1c
Catch
dark slate · dust falling in
Reads as a developer tool and holds up best on a busy wallpaper. The falling dust tells the story without a single sparkle.

Same geometry pushed to the edges with a heavier handle — the clearest read at 16 px, and the only one that survives as a monochrome template.
